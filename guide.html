How to read the screen

What every section, color and label on the live HOT 2 Scanner means, top to bottom. Everything updates by itself every few seconds from 4:00 AM to 8:00 PM ET, Monday to Friday.

📚Key words

These show up everywhere on the screen.

YHOD

Yesterday's high of day: the highest price the stock traded anywhere yesterday, premarket, regular hours or after-hours (4:00 AM–8:00 PM ET). If it went higher after-hours, that higher price is the YHOD. One line, the big one traders watch.

PMHOD

Premarket high of day: the highest price today between 4:00 and 9:29 AM ET. Before 9:30 HOT 2 alerts on YHOD breaks; from 9:30 on it alerts on breaks of today's premarket high.

Breakout

The price going above one of those lines (or above a tight trading range) after being under it.

1-min candle / 1-min volume

How many shares traded in one minute. 100k means 100,000 shares in a single minute, a sign that real money is moving the stock. The scanner checks this while the minute is still forming, not only after it closes.

Float

How many shares are available to trade. Small floats (under 20M, shown in bright white) can move fast. Larger floats are shown dimmer.

▲Top of the screen

● live   ● offline

Top right corner. Green "live" means the screen is getting fresh data. Red "scanner offline" means no update for over 90 seconds, so what you see is old. Don't use it until it turns green again.

🔊 MOST VOLUME RIGHT NOW: FRTT 629,187

The single stock on your screen with the biggest 1-minute volume right now. Its volume number is also highlighted yellow wherever it appears.

⭐ PM HIGH BREAK + 100K

Blinking gold banner (9:30 AM to 8:00 PM). A stock just broke above today's premarket high with 100k+ shares a minute. After 4:00 PM it reads ⭐ DAY HIGH BREAK + 100K: the line becomes today's high from the regular session (9:30–4:00). The top of the screen is kept for this alert only.

🔔 Bells ON

The bells. Gold chime (three bright notes): every time a new gold lights up, a new premarket-high (or after 4 PM, day-high) break on 100k, a re-break after a retest, or a first alert with 100k. Push beeps (two low beeps): a new CONSOLIDATION PUSH (red row) on your screen. Neither repeats for the same alert. Browsers only allow sound after you click the page once, so the first time, click the button (it plays a test chime). Click it again to turn the bells off; your choice is remembered.

🔔 NCPL rang · 08:13:13

Which stock rang. The bar under the bell button shows the stock that just rang the bell, why (gold chime or push beeps, and what set it off), its 1-minute volume and the price. Click the bar to jump to that stock. The same stock gets a shaking bell and a RANG 08:13 tag on its alert card and its board rows for 2 minutes, then a still bell so you can see it rang earlier. Earlier bells lists the last few. Gold = gold chime, red = push beeps.

🎨Alert colors

The whole alert row lights up so you can spot it without reading.

1ST ALERT + 100K

Blinking gold, the big one. It's the stock's first alert of the day (x1) and it's doing 100k+ shares a minute right now. It stops blinking when volume drops under 100k or the stock alerts a second time.

PM HIGH BREAK + 100K

Blinking gold, from 9:30 AM on. The stock just broke above today's premarket high with 100k+ shares a minute, whether it's the 1st alert or the 10th. It stays gold while it holds above with 100k. If it fails and drops back under the premarket high, then breaks it again with 100k (the retest that takes off), it lights gold again. It also shows as a blinking gold banner at the top of the screen (⭐ PM HIGH BREAK + 100K), so you see it even when the stock's row isn't in the alert list. After hours (4:00–8:00 PM) the same thing runs on today's regular-session high instead and reads DAY HIGH BREAK + 100K.

steady gold edge
ABOVE PM HIGH 0.2422 · +13% · since 12:04

From 9:30 to 4:00: the stock is holding ABOVE today's premarket high (after 4:00 PM: ABOVE DAY HIGH, today's 9:30–4:00 high, with a ▲HOD tag), one of the biggest lines to break. The row keeps a solid gold bar on its left (no blinking) and shows how far above it is and since when. In the BREAKING NOW table the stock gets a gold ▲PMH tag. If it falls back under, the chip turns gray (UNDER PM HIGH 1.44 · −1.4%) and the next break with 100k blinks gold again.

purple glow

The stock is doing 100k+ shares a minute right now (any alert, first or later). It goes back to normal when it cools off.

amber

A fresh face: first alert of the day with real volume. Stays highlighted until it alerts again.

CONSOLIDATION PUSH

Red row = CONSOLIDATION PUSH. The stock was sitting quiet in a tight trading range (its "box", with the 1-minute and 5-minute ranges lined up), then a 1-minute candle with 100k+ shares closed right on top of it: it is pushing out of the consolidation. The stock's own row turns red with a red CONSOLIDATION PUSH tag for 15 minutes, and the push bell plays two low beeps.

IN THE BOX

Dashed gray outline = IN THE BOX. The stock is sitting quiet in its consolidation right now: its last 1-minute candle closed inside both its 1-minute and its 5-minute box. The tag shows the box range and since when. It is a watch-this-one sign, not a buy sign: a stock coming out of a box can move big either way. If it pushes out the top on 100k it turns red (CONSOLIDATION PUSH) and beeps; if it closes outside the box the outline goes away. No bell. Checked once a minute, for the stocks the push alert is watching (first alerts with a 100k candle).

plain

A normal alert with nothing special going on right now.

FRESH 9:30+

From the 9:30 open, a second kind of row (green). The first 1-minute candle of the regular session with 100k+ shares, green, stock up 10%+, and price still under PMHOD (today's premarket high). It counts even if the stock alerted in premarket, because the open is a new start. One FRESH per stock per day. It shows the trigger candle (e.g. "232k in 9:34") and how far it is to PMHOD, and it comes off once the price breaks above PMHOD (that's when HOT 2 takes over).

Top 5 by volume

At most 5 rows show, the ones with the most 1-minute volume right now on top. A brand-new FRESH row is always shown for its first 3 minutes even if it's not top 5 by volume. "Nothing right now" means nothing qualifies at the moment.

The top 5 stocks with the most action right now, whether or not they broke a line.

status

Total shares traded today.

from HOD · vs YHOD
