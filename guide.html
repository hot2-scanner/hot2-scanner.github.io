How to read the screen

What every section, color and label on the live HOT 2 Scanner means, top to bottom. Everything updates by itself every few seconds from 4:00 AM to 8:00 PM ET, Monday to Friday.

📚Key words

These show up everywhere on the screen.

YHOD

Yesterday's high of day: the highest price the stock traded anywhere yesterday, premarket, regular hours or after-hours (4:00 AM–8:00 PM ET). If it went higher after-hours, that higher price is the YHOD. One line, the big one traders watch.

PMHOD

Premarket high of day: the highest price today between 4:00 and 9:29 AM ET. Before 9:30 HOT 2 alerts on YHOD breaks; from 9:30 on it alerts on breaks of today's premarket high.

Breakout

The price going above one of those lines (or above a tight trading range) after being under it.

1-min candle / 1-min volume

How many shares traded in one minute. 100k means 100,000 shares in a single minute, a sign that real money is moving the stock. The scanner checks this while the minute is still forming, not only after it closes.

Float

How many shares are available to trade. Small floats (under 20M, shown in bright white) can move fast. Larger floats are shown dimmer.

▲Top of the screen

● live   ● offline

Top right corner. Green "live" means the screen is getting fresh data. Red "scanner offline" means no update for over 90 seconds, so what you see is old. Don't use it until it turns green again. The page also updates itself when a new version of the scanner comes out: a green bar says so, and one click turns the bells back on.

The single stock on your screen with the biggest 1-minute volume right now. Its volume number is also highlighted yellow wherever it appears.

⭐ GOLD 09:45 · again 10:18

The gold star sits on the stock itself (9:30 AM to 8:00 PM), on its alert card and its board row. It means the stock broke above PMHOD (today's premarket high) with 100k+ shares a minute. It shows the first gold time today and the latest re-break (gold re-alerts on a retest). Bright yellow = gold right now; dim = it was gold earlier today. After 4:00 PM the line becomes today's high from the regular session (9:30–4:00). The bell bar at the top tells you when gold rang.

🔔 Bells ON

The bells. Gold chime (three bright notes): every time a new gold lights up, a new premarket-high (or after 4 PM, day-high) break on 100k, a re-break after a retest, or a first alert with 100k. Push sound (two soft rising notes with a little echo): a new CONSOLIDATION PUSH (red row) on your screen. Neither repeats for the same alert. Browsers only allow sound after you click the page once, so the first time, click the button (it plays a test chime). Click it again to turn the bells off; your choice is remembered.

🔔 NCPL rang · 08:13:13

Which stock rang. The bar under the bell button shows the stock that just rang the bell, why (gold chime or push sound, and what set it off), its 1-minute volume and the price. Click the bar to jump to that stock. The same stock gets a shaking bell and a RANG 08:13 tag on its alert card and its board rows for 2 minutes, then a still bell so you can see it rang earlier. Earlier bells lists the last few. Gold = gold chime, red = push sound.

🎨Alert colors

The whole alert row lights up so you can spot it without reading.

1ST ALERT + 100K

Blinking gold, the big one. It's the stock's first alert of the day (x1) and it's doing 100k+ shares a minute right now. It blinks for about 10 seconds, then keeps a steady gold glow; the gold goes away when volume drops under 100k or the stock alerts a second time.

PM HIGH BREAK + 100K

Blinking gold, from 9:30 AM on. The stock just broke above today's premarket high with 100k+ shares a minute, whether it's the 1st alert or the 10th. It stays gold while it holds above with 100k. If it fails and drops back under the premarket high, then breaks it again with 100k (the retest that takes off), it lights gold again. New gold blinks for about 10 seconds, then keeps a steady glow, and the stock gets its ⭐ GOLD star. After hours (4:00–8:00 PM) the same thing runs on today's regular-session high instead and reads DAY HIGH BREAK + 100K.

CONSOLIDATION PUSH

Red row = CONSOLIDATION PUSH. The stock was sitting quiet in a tight trading range (its "box", with the 1-minute and 5-minute ranges lined up), then a 1-minute candle with 100k+ shares closed right on top of it: it is pushing out of the consolidation. The stock's own row turns red with a red CONSOLIDATION PUSH tag for 15 minutes, and the push bell plays the push sound (two soft rising notes).

IN THE BOX

Dashed gray outline = IN THE BOX. The stock is sitting quiet in its consolidation right now: its last 1-minute candle closed inside both its 1-minute and its 5-minute box. The tag shows the box range and since when. If it sits in only one of the two boxes there's no outline, just a small tag: 1M BOX 0.7667 – 0.7962 or 5M BOX. The outline is only for both boxes, the tightest setup. It is a watch-this-one sign, not a buy sign: a stock coming out of a box can move big either way. If it pushes out the top on 100k it turns red (CONSOLIDATION PUSH) and plays the push sound; if it closes outside the box the outline goes away. No bell. Checked once a minute, for the stocks the push alert is watching (first alerts with a 100k candle).
