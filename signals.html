ON THE SCANNER

YHOD break

Breaking yesterday's high

WHAT IT IS
Price goes above YHOD (blue): yesterday's highest price, premarket and after-hours included.
WHAT TO LOOK FOR
A close above the blue line with volume many times a normal minute.
WHAT SOMETIMES HAPPENS
The blue line turns into a floor. VNRX later reached about $0.2757 that morning.
WHAT CAN GO WRONG
It pokes above the line and closes back under it: a fakeout.
REAL EXAMPLE
Real alert: VNRX 11-13. A fresh alert stays highlighted until it alerts again.
ON THE SCANNER

GOLD

First break of the premarket high

WHAT IT IS
The first break above today's premarket high (magenta) with 100,000+ shares trading in a minute.
WHAT TO LOOK FOR
Price clearing the magenta line, a big green volume bar, the gold star on the scanner.
WHAT SOMETIMES HAPPENS
It dips back to the line, holds it, and pushes again. VIVS later reached about $0.38.
WHAT CAN GO WRONG
It falls back under the line. POAS 11-12 ran to about $3.60, then slid back to $2.82.
REAL EXAMPLE
Real alert: VIVS 11-19. The row blinks gold on the scanner.
ON THE SCANNER

100K / MIN (purple)

100,000+ shares in one minute, right now

WHAT IT IS
The stock is trading 100,000+ shares in a single minute right now. The row glows purple.
WHAT TO LOOK FOR
The first 100k minute right at a breakout: the 1ST 100K BREAKOUT tag.
WHAT SOMETIMES HAPPENS
Buyers keep coming and the stock holds above the line it just broke.
WHAT CAN GO WRONG
It is a one-minute burst. When the 100k cools, the purple goes away and price can fade with it.
REAL EXAMPLE
Real alert: GMM 11-16. The row glows purple while the 100k minute is live.
ON THE SCANNER

1 MIN / 5 MIN box breakout

Consolidation box breakout

WHAT IT IS
Price goes quiet in a tight range. The toolkit draws a white box; a green arrow marks a new coil.
WHAT TO LOOK FOR
Smaller candles inside the box, volume drying up, then a candle closing above the top line.
WHAT SOMETIMES HAPPENS
Price leaves the box and runs to the next line above.
WHAT CAN GO WRONG
It breaks out, spikes and falls back in. GMEX 11-12 hit $0.85 and gave it all back.
REAL EXAMPLE
Real alert: SCNI 11-18. Red row = a 100k candle closing on top of its box.
ON THE SCANNER

Under the lines

Heavy volume before the breakout

WHAT IT IS
Stocks NOT on HOT 2 yet doing 100k+ shares a minute while still UNDER YHOD (the premarket high after 9:30).
WHAT TO LOOK FOR
Volume building under the line, and how far it still has to go to reach it.
WHAT SOMETIMES HAPPENS
It reaches the line, breaks it, and shows up on HOT 2.
WHAT CAN GO WRONG
Volume dries up before it gets there. It is a watch list, not an alert.
REAL EXAMPLE
Real row: VWAV 11-20, not on HOT 2 yet, 100k+ a minute under the line.
IN THE THINKORSWIM TOOLKIT

Pattern A

The pullback that holds halfway

WHAT IT IS
2 or more green candles, then a red candle that holds the halfway line of that run. A white arrow marks it when the red candle closes.
WHAT TO LOOK FOR
Big arrow = above the line (YHOD / premarket high). Small arrow = still under it.
WHAT SOMETIMES HAPPENS
The pullback holds and the next green candle takes out the high.
WHAT CAN GO WRONG
The next candle breaks the halfway line and the run is over.
IN THE THINKORSWIM TOOLKIT

Volume surge

2.5x the volume of the candle before

WHAT IT IS
A white arrow in the volume pane: this candle traded 2.5x the volume of the one before.
WHAT TO LOOK FOR
The surge on a green candle that is breaking a line. Real money stepping in.
WHAT SOMETIMES HAPPENS
More big green bars follow and the move keeps going.
WHAT CAN GO WRONG
One huge bar and done: volume fades and price slides back.
