100K FLAG

The 100k candle

A purple glow when a stock is doing 100,000+ shares in its 1-minute candle. Gold when that happens on a stock's very first alert of the day, after the open every time it breaks above the premarket high on 100k, and after 4:00 PM every time it breaks above the day's high on 100k (a failed break that retests and goes again lights gold again). CONSOLIDATION PUSH

Out of the box on volume

A stock sitting quiet in a tight range, then one 1-minute candle with 100k+ shares closes right on top of it. Its row turns red, and you hear the push sound. Gold breaks get their own chime, so you know which one it is without looking.

UNDER THE LINES

Early momentum

Stocks doing 100k+ shares a minute right now while still under the line (yesterday's high before the open, today's premarket high after), so you see them before they break it and often before they show up on the main list. Each row shows when it last hit 100k, and whether it's in its 1-minute / 5-minute box or just broke out. Top 5 by volume.
